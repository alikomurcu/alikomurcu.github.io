About Suit AI

Suit AI is an innovative mobile application that leverages artificial intelligence to provide users with a realistic virtual try-on experience. Using advanced computer vision and machine learning algorithms, the app allows users to see how different clothing items would look on them without physically trying them on.

The application is built using React Native and Expo, ensuring cross-platform compatibility for both iOS and Android devices. It's designed to be intuitive and user-friendly, making fashion shopping more convenient and engaging.
